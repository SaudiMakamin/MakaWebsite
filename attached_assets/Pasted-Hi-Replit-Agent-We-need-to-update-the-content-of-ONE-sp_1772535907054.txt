<h2>Second Notice</h2>
<h3>Urgent Requirement to Update Shareholder Information and Ensure Governance Compliance</h3>

<p>
Pursuant to Board Resolution No. (8/2025) concerning the activation and update of the Shareholder Register, and Board Resolution No. (9/2025) relating to governance compliance and formal verification procedures, and with reference to the launch of the Shareholder Information Update page on 21 July 2025, Makamin Saudi Holding Company for Oil &amp; Gas Services hereby informs all shareholders that, to date, no complete updates meeting regulatory requirements have been received through the designated portal.
</p>

<p>
Continued reliance on outdated shareholder records does not align with compliance and governance standards, particularly in light of observable discrepancies in certain ownership and attendance records. This necessitates the immediate completion of data updates and formal verification procedures.
</p>

<p>
The Company confirms that the update process is intended to align the existing shareholder register with regulatory requirements and to verify any discrepancies, without prejudice to legally established rights supported by valid documentation.
</p>

<h4>Accordingly, updating shareholder information is mandatory to ensure:</h4>

<ul>
<li>Accuracy of the shareholder register</li>
<li>Integrity of voting and representation procedures</li>
<li>Compliance with governance and regulatory obligations</li>
<li>Protection of lawful shareholder rights</li>
</ul>

<p>
No amendment or ownership claim shall be reflected in the shareholder register unless formal verification procedures are completed in accordance with the Company’s Articles of Association and applicable regulations.
</p>

<p>
All shareholders are required to promptly access the Shareholder Update portal and submit the required information and supporting documents at:
<br/>
<strong>www.makamin.com.sa/update-shareholder</strong>
</p>

<p>
For assistance:
<br/>📧 report@makamin.com.sa
<br/>📧 info@makamin.com.sa
</p>
